Delete your A1 Flow account

KINDIX DEVS

Option 1: delete it in the app (fastest)

  1. Open A1 Flow and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Under Account, tap Delete account and confirm.

Your online account and your backed-up progress are deleted. Progress stored on your phone stays until you reset the app or uninstall it.

Option 2: ask us by email

If you can no longer open the app, email kindixdevs@gmail.com from the email address you used for your account, with the subject "Delete my A1 Flow account". We will delete the account for you and confirm by email.

What is deleted, and when

We delete or anonymise your personal information within 30 days. This includes your account details (name, email, account ID) and your saved progress. We may keep limited records where the law requires it, for example proof of a purchase. Backups are removed on their normal schedule.

Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not cancel a Premium subscription. Cancel it in your Google Play or Apple App Store subscription settings first, so you are not charged again.
