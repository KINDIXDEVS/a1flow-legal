A1 Flow: Privacy Policy

Last updated 4 October 2026

Who we are

A1 Flow ("the app", "we", "us") is a study app with quizzes, lessons, riddles and mock exams. It is made and published by KINDIX DEVS, based in Ghana. We are responsible for the personal information described in this policy.

Please read this policy together with our Terms of Use.

The short version

You can use A1 Flow with an account. We collect only what we need to run the app: your account details, your study progress and some technical information. The free version shows advertising. Premium is a paid upgrade with no ads. We do not sell your personal information, and you can delete your account and data at any time.

Information you give us

• Account details: when you sign in with Google, Apple or email, we receive your name, email address and a unique account ID. If you sign in with Google or Apple, we may also receive your profile picture. We do not see your Google or Apple password. If you use email sign-in, your password is handled securely by our sign-in provider.

• A nickname or first name, if you choose to add one in Settings.

• Messages you send us, such as emails to our support address.

Information created when you use the app

The app records your study activity: your streak, coins, diamonds, scores, grades, questions answered, badges, lessons read and your settings (such as daily goal and round length). This is stored on your device and, when you are signed in, saved to your account so you can restore it on a new device.

Information collected automatically

• Device and app information: device model, operating system, app version, language and time zone.

• Crash and performance information, to find and fix problems.

• Internet address (IP), from which an approximate location such as country or city can be worked out.

• Advertising identifier and ad interaction data, in the free version, when ads are shown.

• Subscription status: whether you have Premium and when it ends.

Information we do not collect

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone data, or payment card details. The app does not ask for these permissions.

How we use information

• To create and manage your account and keep you signed in.

• To save and restore your progress and show your statistics and badges.

• To provide, maintain, secure and improve the app and its content.

• To show advertising in the free version, and to manage Premium.

• To respond to your questions and requests.

• To prevent fraud and misuse, and to meet our legal duties.

Advertising

The free version of A1 Flow shows ads provided by third-party advertising networks, such as Google AdMob. These partners may use your advertising identifier and ad interaction data to show and measure ads. Where the law requires it, we ask for your consent before showing personalised ads, and you can choose non-personalised ads.

You can limit personalised ads at any time in your device settings, for example by resetting or deleting your advertising ID. Premium users do not see ads.

Payments

Premium purchases are processed by the Google Play Store or the Apple App Store. We never see or store your card or bank details. We receive confirmation that a purchase or subscription is active, and we may use a subscription management service to check that status. Your payments are also governed by the store's own terms and privacy policy.

Who we share information with

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with:

• Service providers who help us run the app, such as sign-in, cloud hosting and storage, crash reporting and subscription management. They may use it only to provide their service to us.

• Advertising partners, as described above, in the free version.

• The Google Play Store and Apple App Store, for downloads and purchases.

• Authorities or other parties when the law requires it, or to protect rights, safety and security.

• A buyer or successor, if the app or our business is transferred, with the same protection for your information.

International transfers

Our service providers may process information on servers outside Ghana, including in other countries. Where we do this, we take steps to protect your information as required by applicable law.

How long we keep information

We keep your account and progress for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we delete or anonymise your personal information within 30 days, except where we must keep some records to meet legal duties, such as proof of a purchase. Backups are removed on their normal schedule.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account and its data from Settings in the app. You can also email us at the address below from the email used for your account, and we will delete it for you. Uninstalling the app removes the data stored on your device but does not delete your online account.

Age and children

A1 Flow is intended for people aged 13 and over. Younger students may use it only with a parent or guardian setting up and supervising the account, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without that permission. Users under 18 should use the app with the knowledge of a parent or guardian. If you believe a child has given us personal information without permission, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections and access controls. No system is completely secure, so please keep your device and account details safe and use a screen lock.

Your rights and choices

You may ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information, to object to or limit how we use it, or to withdraw your consent at any time. You may also ask for a copy of your information. These rights come from the Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843) of Ghana and, where they apply to you, other laws such as the GDPR. To use them, contact us. You also have the right to complain to the Data Protection Commission of Ghana or your local data protection authority.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. The date at the top shows when it was last changed. If the changes are important, we will tell you in the app or by email before they take effect.
